What I Work On

My work sits at the intersection of computational tools, regulatory science, and traditional bench research. Here's where I spend most of my time.

🤖

AI-Powered R&D Systems

Building and deploying LLM-based tools for literature synthesis, experimental design, and formulation discovery. Applied Python-driven optimization to fermentation processes, contributing to a 200% production increase.

LLMs Python MLOps Bioprocess

FDA Regulatory & Clinical Trials

Designed and managed a Phase II clinical trial for a botanical drug targeting metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH). Secured NDI 1286 notification and navigated cross-border regulatory pathways across the US, Taiwan, and Japan.

FDA IND NDI Phase II GRAS

Production-Scale Biotechnology

Scaling precision fermentation for bioactive compounds, managing quality assurance from raw materials to finished products, and migrating 700+ QC documents to SAP ERP. Led teams of 8+ across R&D, QA, and business functions.

Fermentation SAP ERP QA/QC Scale-up

Functional Nutrition Innovation

Developing evidence-backed nutraceuticals from medicinal fungi including Antrodia cinnamomea and Sanghuangporus sanghuang. Our work on cognitive function ingredients was recognized as Nutra-Ingredients Asia's Ingredient of the Year (2024).
